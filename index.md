# Ballast — Support

Ballast is a strength log built around one idea: every set is already filled in at the
weight and reps you did last time, so logging it is **one tap**.

**Questions, bugs, or feature requests:**
[developer.cloud26@gmail.com](mailto:developer.cloud26@gmail.com)

I read every message, and most get a reply within two business days. If you're reporting a
bug, telling me your iPhone model and iOS version helps a lot.

There is no account to recover and no password to reset — Ballast has neither.

## Common questions

### My workouts aren't appearing on my other device

Ballast syncs through **your own private iCloud**. Both devices need to be signed in to the
same Apple Account, with iCloud sync on in **Me → iCloud sync** — it is on unless you have
turned it off. The row tells you what it is doing, and a change to it takes effect the next
time Ballast is opened.

A device that has been offline catches up the next time it has a connection. If iCloud isn't
available, Ballast works exactly the same on that device alone.

### I bought Pro but it isn't unlocked

Open **Me** and tap **Restore purchase**, under YOUR DATA. Pro is a one-time purchase tied to
your Apple Account, so it restores on any device signed into the same account, and you will
never be charged twice. Family Sharing is on.

If it still doesn't unlock, check you're signed into the same Apple Account that made the
purchase, then email me.

### Does Pro renew? Will I be charged again?

No. Ballast Pro is a **one-time purchase**, not a subscription. There is nothing to cancel
and nothing that renews.

### What's free, and what's in Pro?

Logging is free, permanently: unlimited workouts, three plans, every set type, swapping an
exercise for the day, the rest timer, plate math, warm-up sets, supersets, a note on any
lift, the Apple Watch app, Home and Lock Screen widgets, Siri and Shortcuts, Apple Health,
and CSV export and import.

Pro adds automatic progression — the weight goes up when you have earned it, and a deload
that asks first when you stall — reps in reserve, unlimited plans and custom exercises, and
your full history and charts instead of the last three months.

### Using the Apple Watch app

Open Ballast on your iPhone first, so the watch has a plan to show. Today's workout then
appears on the watch. Turn the Digital Crown to change a weight — for a barbell or dumbbell
lift it only offers weights your plates and dumbbells can make — and tap the check to log
the set.

Sets you log on the watch are kept on the watch until your iPhone receives them, so nothing
is lost if your phone is in a locker. The corner of the screen shows how many are waiting.

### The rest timer, and its notification

Rest starts on its own when you log a set, and runs on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic
Island, so the phone can go back in your pocket. The "Rest finished" alert can be turned off
in **Me → Reminders**; the Lock Screen timer in iOS **Settings → Ballast → Live Activities**.

### I turned on Apple Health but nothing appears there

Ballast adds a workout to Health when you **finish** a session, not while it is running. iOS
does not tell an app whether you granted permission — by design — so to check or change it
go to **Health app → Sharing → Apps → Ballast**.

Ballast only writes to Health. It reads nothing from it.

### Siri and the Action Button

Say **"Start my workout in Ballast"** to open today's session, or **"Export my Ballast log"**
to hand your full history to a shortcut as a CSV file. Both are also in the Shortcuts app,
and Start Workout can be assigned to the Action Button. Exporting only works on an unlocked
iPhone.

### Moving my log from another app

**Me → Import CSV** reads a CSV file and merges it in — it adds the sessions you don't have
and changes nothing that is already there. A file exported from Ballast imports as it is.
From another app, rename its columns so the file has **Date**, **Exercise**, **Weight**,
**Unit** and **Reps**, and it will import too.

### Switching between lb and kg — will I lose anything?

No. Every set keeps the weight and unit it was logged in, and switching in **Me → Units**
changes what you see, never your history.

### How do I delete a workout?

Open it from **History**, scroll to the bottom of the summary, and tap **Delete workout**.
The sets go with it, and your plan stays as it is.

### How do I delete everything?

Delete the app to remove everything stored on the iPhone. If iCloud sync was on, remove the
iCloud copy in iOS **Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage**. Workouts
already added to Apple Health are yours and stay there until you delete them in Health.

If you want a copy first, **Me → Export CSV** writes your whole history to a file. It is free.

### Is this coaching or medical advice?

No. Ballast is a log that does the arithmetic on your own numbers — when to add weight, when
to back off. It is not a medical device and gives no medical advice. If something about your
training or your health concerns you, speak to a qualified professional.

## Privacy

Ballast collects nothing. No account, no server, no analytics, no third-party code. Your
training is stored on your iPhone and in your own private iCloud, which we hold no key to
and cannot read.
